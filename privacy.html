Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the application Verdamed-finance-tracker (Google Cloud project klinik-awfa-connection), operated by Kumpulan Ikram Health Terengganu Sdn. Bhd. (Klinik Awfa), website klinikawfa.com.

1. What data we access

This application accesses Google Workspace data through the following APIs, strictly for the clinic's internal accounting and administration:

  • Gmail (read, send, modify) — to receive payment notifications, invoices, and bank statements, and to send accounting reports.
  • Google Sheets — to maintain the clinic's financial ledgers (Money In & Money Out records).
  • Google Drive — to store and organize accounting documents (invoices, receipts, statements).
  • Google Calendar and Contacts — for scheduling and contact reference of clinic operations.
  • Google Docs — to generate accounting documents.

2. How data is used

Data accessed through these APIs is used exclusively for:

  • Recording and reconciling clinic income and expenses against bank statements.
  • Tracking supplier invoices and payments.
  • Generating internal financial reports.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party, except as required by law. All data remains within the clinic's own Google Workspace accounts.

4. Data retention & deletion

Financial records are retained as required by Malaysian law for business accounting. You may revoke this application's access to your Google data at any time via your Google Account permissions page. Upon revocation, the application immediately loses all access to your data.

For any questions regarding this policy, contact us at klinikawfahq@gmail.com.
